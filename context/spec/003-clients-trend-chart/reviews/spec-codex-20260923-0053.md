### F1: Keyboard selection can highlight the wrong month
- severity: major
- where: technical-considerations.md:§3 R-4
- problem: The plan explicitly accepts a tint on the hovered month while the panel and announcement describe February. This violates FR5’s requirement that the tint and panel follow the keyboard-selected month.
- evidence: “The tint stays on the hovered month until the pointer moves; accepted as low severity.”
- fix: Drive the tint from the widget’s selected index too. Add a test that hovers June, then focuses the chart and verifies February’s tint, panel and announcement.

### F2: Touch dismissal excludes areas that must dismiss
- severity: major
- where: technical-considerations.md:§2.7 Outside tap
- problem: FR4 requires tapping anywhere outside the bars to dismiss the panel. The proposed listener dismisses only outside the widget, leaving taps on its legend, axis or padding unhandled.
- evidence: Functional requirement: “tapping anywhere outside the bars dismisses it”; implementation: “closing when the target is outside the widget root.”
- fix: Define the bar hit area and dismiss taps outside that area, including inside the widget. Test legend and card-padding taps.

### F3: The proposed layout does not preserve plot height
- severity: major
- where: technical-considerations.md:§2.6 The container
- problem: A fixed-height flex column with a flexible plot and an HTML legend reduces the plot’s height when the legend wraps. Moving the legend outside Recharts alone does not satisfy FR8’s equal-height requirement.
- evidence: The plot uses “flex: 1 1 auto” and the legend “flex: none”; §2.5 identifies legend wrapping at 375 px as consuming additional height.
- fix: Give the plot a fixed height independent of the legend and allow the container to grow, or reserve the same legend height at both widths. Verify using the actual legend at 375 and 1440 px.

## Verdict
DO NOT SHIP

The technical plan contradicts required selection, touch-dismissal and responsive-layout behavior.
