### F1: Screen-reader deduplication requirement conflicts with two reading paths
- severity: major
- where: functional-spec.md:FR6
- problem: The spec requires both keyboard announcements and a readable summary table, but also forbids any month's figures being announced twice. QA could interpret this as prohibiting the required second reading path.
- evidence: “two ways” exposes the same figures twice, while the acceptance criterion says “no month's figures are announced twice.”
- fix: Limit deduplication to each keyboard navigation event; explicitly allow rereading figures through the summary table.

### F2: Initial keyboard selection and focus behavior are undefined
- severity: major
- where: functional-spec.md:FR5
- problem: The chart receives focus without a defined active month, so the first Right press has no defined result. It is also unclear whether the outline stays on the chart or moves to individual bars.
- evidence: “outline appears on the chart” precedes “outline moves to the next month,” without an initial month or re-entry rule.
- fix: Define February as the initial active month, whether its panel opens on focus, the focus indicator's location, and whether re-entry preserves the active month.

### F3: Tapping another month has contradictory outcomes
- severity: minor
- where: functional-spec.md:FR4
- problem: Tapping another month can mean replacing the current panel or dismissing it without opening another.
- evidence: “tapping a month shows its panel” conflicts with “tapping elsewhere or another month dismisses it.”
- fix: Specify that tapping another month replaces the panel with that month's figures; tapping outside dismisses the panel and tint.

### F4: Keyboard tooltip dismissal is unspecified
- severity: minor
- where: functional-spec.md:FR4–FR5
- problem: Keyboard navigation opens a panel, but no requirement says what happens when focus leaves or Escape is pressed. An implementation could leave a stale panel covering the chart after Tab reaches the table.
- evidence: Dismissal is specified only for pointer exit and tapping elsewhere.
- fix: Require blur to clear the panel and tint, and define Escape dismissal while keeping the active month.

### F5: Scale acceptance criteria allow incompatible results
- severity: minor
- where: functional-spec.md:FR2
- problem: “Round number” has no testable definition, and allowing the maximum to equal the scale ceiling contradicts requiring spare room.
- evidence: The top must be “at or above the largest month” while every bar must fit “with room to spare.”
- fix: Define allowed tick increments and whether the ceiling must strictly exceed the maximum; give an expected ceiling for the supplied maximum of 350.

## Verdict
SHIP WITH FIXES

Resolve the accessibility and interaction ambiguities so implementation and QA agree on observable behavior.
